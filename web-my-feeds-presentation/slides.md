<div class="eyebrow">Why My Feeds</div>

<p class="statement">Everything you follow, <em>in one feed.</em></p>

<p class="lead mt">My Feeds watches the topics you care about across YouTube, X, Reddit and more, sums up what's new, and lets you watch and read it all right here.</p>

<div class="grid-3" style="margin-top: 48px;">
  <div class="card"><h3>Watches for you</h3><p>Collections check your sources on a schedule, so you don't have to open every app.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>Sums it up</h3><p>Posts arrive with a short summary, and videos with their key moments.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>Keeps you in one place</h3><p>Watch, read, like and save without jumping between apps.</p></div>
</div>

<!--
The problem is simple: the people you follow are spread across many apps.
My Feeds brings them together. It watches your sources for you, summarises what's new, and lets you read and watch without leaving the app.
-->

<div class="eyebrow">How it works</div>

# Three quick steps

<div class="grid-3 mt">
  <div class="card accent"><div class="num">1</div><h3>Build a collection</h3><p>A collection follows one topic. Name it after what you want to follow.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="num">2</div><h3>Add sources</h3><p>The channels, accounts and communities this collection should watch.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><div class="num">3</div><h3>Read &amp; watch</h3><p>New posts land in your Feeds, ready to watch and read without leaving.</p></div>
</div>

<p class="lead mt" style="font-size: 20px;">The first time you open My Feeds, a short wizard walks you through all three. Reopen it any time with the <b>?</b> button on the Dashboard.</p>

<!--
Here's the whole idea in three steps: build a collection, add sources, then read and watch.
The onboarding wizard covers these when you first sign in, and you can bring it back later from the question mark button on the Dashboard.
-->

    <li><span class="dot">✉</span><span><b>Emails a digest.</b> Your email is added to every new collection.</span></li>

# Most actions stay in My Feeds

<p class="lead">Photos, videos and quoted posts open in full. Saving happens here; posting from your account opens the platform.</p>

<table class="actions mt">
  <thead><tr><th>Platform</th><th>In My Feeds</th><th>Opens the platform</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>X</td><td>Like saves it · Bookmark adds it to Read later</td><td class="out">Reply or repost</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Reddit</td><td>Upvote saves · downvote marks it read · Save adds to Read later</td><td class="out">Comments</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Instagram</td><td>Swipe carousels, play reels · heart saves, bookmark adds to Read later</td><td class="out">Comments</td></tr>
    <tr><td>GitHub</td><td>Read new repos and releases with a summary · Star saves</td><td class="out">Fork</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<!--
Posts open in full inside the app, with their photos and videos.
The rule of thumb: anything that just saves or tidies stays in My Feeds. Anything that posts from your own account, like replying on X or forking on GitHub, opens that app.
-->
